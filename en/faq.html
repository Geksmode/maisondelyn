FAQ

Your questions

How far in advance should I book?

Ideally 6 to 12 months ahead, especially for a Saturday between May and September. Feel free to ask even for a date that is coming up soon.

Is the trial required?

It is strongly recommended: it is when we agree on the look together and I get to know your skin.

Where does the trial take place?

At your home in Paris or at a place we agree on together.

Will the make-up last all day?

Yes, I use long-wear, tear-proof products and leave you a touch-up kit.

I have sensitive skin or allergies. What should I do?

Mention it in your request: I adapt the products and we test them during the trial.

How many people can you do on the wedding day?

It depends on the time of the ceremony. Allow about 45 minutes per guest; for more than 5 people, I can come with an assistant.

Do you travel outside Paris?

Yes, anywhere in Île-de-France and in France on request.

How do I book my date?

Send a request through the form. Your date is reserved once the deposit and signed contract are received.

Another question? Write to me.
